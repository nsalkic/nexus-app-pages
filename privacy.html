NEXUS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

The short version: NEXUS does not collect any data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts on our servers. Everything a family enters stays on their own device.

Who we are

NEXUS is a learning app for children in kindergarten through 3rd grade, made by Nijaz Salkic ("we"). Contact: nsalkicpr@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

  • Child profiles: a first name, last name, grade and avatar that a parent types in. Stored only on the device.
  • Progress: scores, stars, books read and drawings. Stored only on the device.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if a parent chooses it, Apple gives the app an anonymous user ID and, if the parent allows, a first name. These stay on the device and are only used to show the parent is signed in. We never receive them. We do not ask for an email address.

We have no servers that receive this information. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or software development kits that collect data.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No chat, social features or sharing with other users.
  • No use of the camera, photos, location, contacts or microphone.
  • The read-aloud voice is recorded inside the app and works offline.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

NEXUS is designed for children under 13. Because we do not collect, use or disclose personal information from children, no personal information leaves the device. Parent-only areas (sign-in, child settings, the About page and any outside link) are protected by a parent gate.

Parents' choices

Parents can edit or delete a child profile in the app at any time (Who is playing? → Edit), sign out, or delete the app to remove everything. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can stop using it in iPhone Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date. We will not start collecting data without updating this policy and the App Store privacy details first.
