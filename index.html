What's inside

Games, reading, books, art and more. All in one place.

Every mission asks real reading and math questions for your child's grade, with a friendly voice that reads everything out loud.

Soccer

Answer to score. Pick a team and a player, then tap the right answer in the goal to shoot.

NEXUS Rally

Race to the finish. Right answers fuel your kart, so the faster you think, the faster you go.

Bot Builders

Build it, then battle. Each right answer installs a robot part, then your bot is ready to battle.

Books

Read, listen and laugh. Stories that teach character and self-discipline: being kind, sticking with it, telling the truth.

  • Picture books with "Read to me" and word-by-word highlighting.
  • Comics with heroes, robots and space adventures.
  • Audio books to listen to with the read-aloud voice.

Art Studio

Color, trace and draw. Hundreds of coloring pages in themes kids love, plus drawing lessons step by step.

  • Coloring: fill, crayon, paint, magic and stickers.
  • Trace and Free Draw for steady hands.
  • Learn to Draw: one easy step at a time.
  • My Art keeps every finished picture.
