NEXUS Support

NEXUS is a learning world for kids in kindergarten through 3rd grade: Soccer, Bot Builders, NEXUS Rally, Art Studio and Books, with questions matched to each grade.

Need help? Email nsalkicpr@gmail.com. We usually answer within two business days.

Common questions

How do I add or change a child? On "Who is playing?", tap the + card or Edit. A grown-up answers a quick question first.

Do I need an account? No. Tap "Continue without account". Profiles and progress stay on this device. You can also use Sign in with Apple.

How do I change the grade? Who is playing? → Edit → pick the grade. Questions follow Illinois learning standards for that grade.

The voice is quiet or silent. Check the ring/silent switch and the volume. The voice is built in and works offline.

How do I delete our data? Edit a child to remove them, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

Privacy

NEXUS collects no data, shows no ads and has no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
